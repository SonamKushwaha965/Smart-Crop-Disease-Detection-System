🎯 Project Objective

Hamara project Smart Crop Disease Detection System (AgroRS) kisano ki madad ke liye hai:

✓ Patte ki photo se bimari aur keet (pest) detect karta hai
✓ AI Model (TFLite) + Image Processing (hole detection)
✓ 94.7% accuracy se pest pakadta hai
✓ Hindi me upchar batata hai
✓ Live Weather se farming advisory deta hai
✓ Bina internet ke bhi kaam karega

Technology: Python, Flask, TensorFlow Lite, OpenCV...

Disease Library - Hamara AI Kya Pehchanta Hai

Smart Crop Disease Detection System (AgroRS) dwara detect ki jane wali 6 mukhya categories

🍅

Tomato - Early Blight

Fungal Disease

Lakshan: Patte par gol bhure daag, peela ghera.
Khatra: Poora paudha sukh sakta hai.
Upchar: Mancozeb 2.5g + paani 1L - 7 din par spray

🥔

Potato - Late Blight

Sabse Khatarnak

Lakshan: Kale geele daag, safed fungus niche.
Khatra: 2 din me pura khet kharab.
Upchar: Copper Oxychloride 3g/L turant spray

🌾

Wheat - Brown Rust

Common in Bihar

Lakshan: Bhuri powder jaisi, patta peela.
Khatra: Dana chhota ho jata hai.
Upchar: Propiconazole 1ml/L spray

🥬

Cabbage - Diamondback Moth

PEST - Hamara Special

Lakshan: Patte me gol-gol jaali/ched, keeda hara.
Hamara Feature: AI hole detection 94.7% se pakadta hai.
Upchar: Bt bacteria 1g + Neem tel 5ml + Shampoo 1ml / 1L paani, shaam ko spray

🌽

Corn - Leaf Blight

Fungal

Lakshan: Lambe bhure daag, patta sukhna.
Khatra: Bhutta chhota.
Upchar: Zineb 2g/L spray

✅

Healthy Leaf

NO DISEASE

Pehchan: Hara bhara, chamakdar, koi daag/ched nahi.
AI bolega: Swasth - 95%+ confidence.
Salah: Aise hi khyal rakho, weekly scan karo.
